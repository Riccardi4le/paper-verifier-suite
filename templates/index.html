Paper Verifier Suite

Three agents that attack the quality of academic papers from different angles. Each one does a single job, and shows its work step by step.

/arv

Adversarial Reviewer

A hostile peer reviewer. Hunts for p-hacking, impossible means (GRIM), unsupported claims, citation padding and methodology gaps.

Input
arXiv / DOI URL or a PDF
Output
Findings ranked by severity
Review a paper /cg

Citation Genealogy

Follows a claim back through its citation chain to the primary source, and scores how much it drifted at every hop.

Input
A claim and the paper citing it
Output
Citation tree with verdicts
Trace a claim /cpc

Cross-Paper Contradiction

Extracts claims from several papers, clusters them by topic, and diagnoses why papers that disagree actually disagree.

Input
Two or more PDFs
Output
Contradiction map
Compare papers
